Project 01 / Aerodynamics
Featured project

Subsonic
Wind Tunnel

An independent benchtop wind-tunnel build designed around a simple objective: create a compact system that can condition airflow, support repeatable aerodynamic testing, and produce useful force and pressure measurements without access to a full machine shop.

Architecture

Designed the complete flow path

Developed a 12 × 12 in inlet, honeycomb and dual-screen conditioning section, 4:1 contraction, clear acrylic test chamber, diffuser, and inline-fan arrangement around a compact benchtop footprint.

Iteration

Worked through fabrication error

Measured fit-up problems, corrected out-of-square transition geometry, revised fastening strategies, and developed sealing methods around real panel tolerances instead of treating fabrication as ideal.

Validation

Leak-tested assembled sections

Sealed and tested the inlet and contraction individually and after joining them, while revising the fan architecture from suction to push when tooling constraints made the original outlet configuration impractical.
