Featured Project

Subsonic Wind Tunnel

Independent benchtop wind-tunnel build focused on aerodynamic testing, flow conditioning, instrumentation, fabrication, and iterative design under real budget and tool constraints.

Design & fabrication

Designed a compact tunnel with a 12 × 12 in inlet, honeycomb and screen flow conditioning, contraction, clear acrylic test section, diffuser, and inline fan. Components were hand-fabricated using plywood, acrylic, braces, fasteners, epoxy, and sealant.

Engineering iteration

Measured fit-up errors, corrected out-of-square transition geometry, developed sealing strategies for panel tolerances, leak-tested assembled sections, and changed the fan architecture from suction to a push configuration when tooling constraints made the original outlet design impractical.
